✅ DALL·E 3 Prompt for Cover
“Create a calm and uplifting illustrated book cover in portrait orientation for a wellness journal titled 'Harmony Health: Mood & Mental Health Tracker'. Show a cozy indoor space with a table, an open journal, and items like a cup of tea, a candle, or a cozy blanket. Use calming colors like soft lavender, sky blue, and warm peach. Include visual hints of mindfulness or calm, such as gentle light from a window, indoor plants, or a stack of books. The title should appear in large serif and sans-serif fonts at the top, and the subtitle at the bottom should read: ‘A guided journal to track emotions, triggers, mental wellness & self-care’. The illustration style should match relaxing self-care books with a warm and inviting aesthetic.”
